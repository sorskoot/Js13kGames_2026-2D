# Rainbow Merge 🌈🦄

The rainbow has lost its magic colors. Help to bring them back, one merge at a time.

Rainbow Merge is a small puzzle game inspired by 2048. Slide the gems around the board. When two gems with the same color meet, they merge into the next color. Travel from Red to Orange, Yellow, Green, Cyan, Blue, and Purple. Then create a Rainbow, and finally a Unicorn.

Making the Unicorn is your big goal. But the game does not need to stop there. Keep playing and try to make a better score. The board becomes more crowded with every move, so plan your next merge carefully.

## Controls

- Use the **Arrow keys** or **WASD** to move all gems.
- On a touch screen, **swipe** up, down, left, or right.
- Matching gems merge when they are pushed together.
- Use **New Game** when you want to start again.
